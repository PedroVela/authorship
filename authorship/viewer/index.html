Jev

  1. Get a key from one of these. Jev is the same model in all three; they differ in who bills you and who sees the text on the way:
    • TypeSafe AI, the maker, direct.
    • OpenRouter (model typesafe/jev-1.13). The plugin asks for zero data retention and no data collection.
    • The Vercel AI Gateway. The plugin asks for zero data retention.
  2. Add the key to your shell profile (~/.zshrc or ~/.bashrc), so every session has it: 
    export TYPESAFE_API_KEY=your-key     # TypeSafe
export OPENROUTER_API_KEY=your-key   # or OpenRouter
export AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY=your-key   # or Vercel
    Then: authorship classifier use jev, optionally with a model and --provider typesafe|openrouter|vercel_gateway.
  3. Open a new terminal and run authorship restart.
  4. Check it: authorship classifier --test sends one made-up sentence and shows the answer.

With Jev or OpenRouter, the text of your entries goes to that provider. Public disclosure before filing can destroy novelty: decide this with your attorney.

Other settings

  • Turn it off: authorship classifier use off. Tags you type and the deterministic rules keep working.
  • Forget the choice: authorship classifier use auto (Jev if a Jev key is set, else Claude).
  • Environment variables win over the saved choice: AUTHORSHIP_AUTO_BACKEND, AUTHORSHIP_AUTO_MODEL, AUTHORSHIP_JEV_PROVIDER, AUTHORSHIP_JEV_MODEL, AUTHORSHIP_AUTO=0.
  • After any change: authorship restart. The annotator keeps the settings it started with.

How to read this

What you invented
Your claims and what each rests on. Every element says whether it came from you, from Claude, or from you changing something Claude proposed.
Timeline
The record in order, grouped by stage of the work. "Key moments" hides Claude's routine work; "Everything" shows every entry. Open a row for its full text and code change.
Review
Labels the classifier was not sure of, for you to accept, reject or change. Only your answers are evidence; the classifier's labels are opinions.

Who labels the entries

A classifier reads every entry in the background and labels it (problem, idea, decision, claim, stage). By default it is Claude, through your own login, so no new party receives your text. A strip under the tabs appears when that changes: text going to another provider, or labels off or failing.

Symbols

  • Circle: written by you
  • Square: written by Claude
  • Dashed outline: discarded or rejected
  • idea Dashed label: set by the classifier; solid: typed by you or confirmed
  • #4: ledger entry 4; #3.2: option 2 of Claude's reply #3

The badge at the top

Intact: no entry changed since it was written (the ledger is hash-chained). Sealed to #N: an external timestamp covers the record up to that entry. Signed: how many of your entries carry your SSH signature (authorship sign setup). It turns red if any entry was changed.
