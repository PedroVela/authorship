    Your answers

    Choose who labels the entries

    Settings are environment variables, read when the annotator starts. Type the commands in your own terminal, not in Claude Code.

    Claude (default)

    Nothing to set up: it uses the claude command and your existing login. The text goes to Anthropic, which already receives your session.

    Another model: export AUTHORSHIP_AUTO_MODEL=claude-haiku-4-5-20251001 (default claude-sonnet-5).

    Jev

    1. Get a key from TypeSafe AI, or a Vercel AI Gateway key (zero data retention).
    2. Add it to your shell profile (~/.zshrc or ~/.bashrc), so every session has it: 
      export TYPESAFE_API_KEY=your-key
# or, through Vercel:
export AI_GATEWAY_API_KEY=your-key
    3. Open a new terminal and run authorship restart.
    4. Check it: authorship classifier --test sends one made-up sentence and shows the answer.

    With Jev, the text of your entries goes to that provider. Public disclosure before filing can destroy novelty: decide this with your attorney.

    Other settings

    • Force one: export AUTHORSHIP_AUTO_BACKEND=claude (or jev)
    • Turn it off: export AUTHORSHIP_AUTO=0. Tags you type and the deterministic rules keep working.
    • After any change: authorship restart. The annotator keeps the settings it started with.

    How to read this

    Overview
    Your claims and what each rests on. Every element says whether it came from you, from Claude, or from you changing something Claude proposed.
    Timeline
    The record in order, grouped by stage of the work. "Key moments" hides Claude's routine work; "Everything" shows every entry.
    Map
    The same entries as a picture: problems on top, ideas in the middle, decisions and claims below, Claude's code and tests at the bottom. Time runs left to right. Drag the slider to replay how it grew.
    Review
    Labels the classifier was not sure of, for you to accept, reject or change. Only your answers are evidence; the classifier's labels are opinions.

    Who labels the entries

    A classifier reads every entry in the background and labels it (problem, idea, decision, claim, stage). By default it is Claude, through your own login, so no new party receives your text. If you set a Jev key it is Jev, and your entry text goes to that provider. The strip under the tiles says which one is in use; "How to change it" shows the steps.

    Symbols

    • Circle: written by you
    • Square: written by Claude
    • Dashed outline: discarded or rejected
    • idea Dashed label: set by the classifier; solid: typed by you or confirmed
    • #4: ledger entry 4; #3.2: option 2 of Claude's reply #3

    The ledger is append-only and hash-chained: the badge at the top turns red if any entry was changed after it was written.
